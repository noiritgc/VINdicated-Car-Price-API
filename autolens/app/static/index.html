CatBoost learns the logarithm of the CARFAX reference price from vehicle specifications and history. Repeated vehicles are removed before a fixed 70/15/15 train/calibration/test split. The 90% range uses split conformal calibration. Coverage is marginal over similar data, not guaranteed for each vehicle or an unfamiliar make/model. No live market feed is used.
